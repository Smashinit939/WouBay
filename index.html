SO, WHAT IS WOUBAY?

Campus life, with
less hassle.

Think of WouBay as your campus noticeboard, thrift store and rental desk rolled into one. It's a marketplace made for Woxsen students, where useful things find new owners and one-time needs don't have to mean one-time purchases.

NO COMPLICATED GROUP CHATS

How it works.

Four simple steps. From “does anyone have one?” to “sorted.”

01
🗒️

Request an item

Tell the campus what you need. Sellers can spot your request and reach out.

02
🔎

List or discover

Add photos and a price, or browse items students have listed for sale or rent.

03
💬

Connect and agree

Message through the platform, ask questions and settle on the details.

04
🤝

Meet on campus

Choose a public campus meetup spot, exchange the item and leave a rating.

↗ Renting follows the same flow — the owner sets a price per day and you agree on a return date.
